# Store policy

## Cancellations
- An order can be cancelled while it is placed or processing. Once it has shipped it cannot be cancelled, but it can be returned after delivery.
- A cancelled order is refunded in full to the original payment method within 5 business days.

## Returns
- Items can be returned within {return_window_days} days of delivery.
- Some items cannot be returned for hygiene or safety reasons, such as earbuds, socks, bedding, gift cards and digital products. The order details show which items are returnable.
- Each order can have one return request. It can include several items, and all units of each chosen item are returned.
- Refunds under ${auto_approve_refund_limit:.0f} are approved automatically. Larger refunds are checked by our team within 2 business days.
- Approved refunds go to the original payment method within 5 business days.

## Shipping
- Orders ship within 1 to 2 business days with DHL, UPS or FedEx.
- Delivery takes 2 to 5 business days after shipping. Each order shows its tracking events.
- If a parcel is more than 3 days late or arrives damaged, the customer can open a support ticket.

## Support
- A human agent replies by email within 1 business day.
- A customer can open at most {max_tickets_per_day} support tickets in 24 hours.
